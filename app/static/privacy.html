Life Inbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Life Inbox is a class project. The course instructor runs it so students in the class can capture notes, links and files and organize them later. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect when you sign in

You sign in with Google. We receive and store your name, your email address and your Google account ID. We never see your Google password, and we do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or Calendar.

What you add

The app stores what you put into it:

  • notes you type, and links you save (the app downloads the linked page to read its text)
  • PDFs, images and voice recordings you upload, plus the text extracted from them
  • tasks, topics, dates and corrections you make, and the app's suggested organization of your items

Who can see your inbox

Only you. Each account sees only its own items, tasks, search results and answers; other students cannot see yours. The instructor operates the service and has technical access to the database and stored files, and will only look at your content to fix a problem you report.

Services that handle your data

  • Render runs the app and stores your uploaded files.
  • Neon hosts the database that holds your account and items.
  • Google handles sign-in. The app page also loads fonts from Google Fonts.
  • OpenAI, only if the instructor turns on AI features. Then the text of your items is sent to OpenAI to organize your inbox, answer your questions, and transcribe voice notes. OpenAI's API terms say API data is not used to train their models by default. When AI features are off, all organizing and answering happens inside the app.
  • Anthropic, only if a Claude API key is set instead. Then the text of your items is sent to Anthropic's Claude to organize your inbox and answer your questions. Voice notes are not sent to Anthropic. Anthropic's commercial terms say API data is not used to train their models by default.

Cookies

The app sets one signed cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie during Google sign-in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your content with anyone outside the services listed above.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item at any time in the app; deleting an item also deletes its uploaded file. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email the contact below and it will be removed.
