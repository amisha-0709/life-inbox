Google Calendar

Meetings from your Google Calendar show up as tasks on the day they happen. Life Inbox only reads your calendar; it never changes it.

Where do I find the address?
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer and click the gear icon, then Settings.
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.

Keep this address private: anyone who has it can see your calendar. Life Inbox stores it only on this computer.
